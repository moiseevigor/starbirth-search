## L · Literature survey

*2026-09-26 · verdict: **confirmed***

**Question.** Has anyone caught a starless core turning into a protostar, and what should the birth look like?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** If birth were observable in archival IR data, the literature would contain either a detection or a model predicting a prompt (years) mid-IR brightening at second-core formation.

**Test.** 36 papers read in full (birth physics, first-core candidates, lifetimes, Hi-GAL, outbursts, survey methods) into the research vault, with a synthesis.

**Result.** No published detection. Every collapse model keeps the newborn inside an opaque envelope: luminosity grows over ~1e2-1e4 yr and near-IR light escapes only after 2e4-1.4e5 yr (Masunaga & Inutsuka 2000); 'no immediate change in the SED' (Young 2023). Accretion bursts of existing protostars recur every ~0.4-2 kyr and mimic a turn-on.

**What could still be wrong.** No model gives the observable light curve over the first 10-1e3 yr; outflow cavities could open a faster mid-IR path. Lifetimes are uncertain by factors of 2-3.

**Next.** Quantify expected births vs burst impostors for the Hi-GAL sample (E-1).

## E0 · Synthetic change-point calibration

*2026-09-26 · verdict: **confirmed***

**Question.** Can a change-point test recover a persistent step in WISE-like cadence?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** A weighted two-level BIC scan detects steps of >= 1 sigma per exposure with near-zero false alarms on 21 epochs x 12 exposures.

**Test.** 2,000 synthetic light curves per amplitude, Gaussian noise, random onset epoch; detection = delta-BIC >= 20 and positive step.

**Result.** 0% false alarms at 0 sigma, 96% detection at 1 sigma, ~100% at >= 1.5 sigma. 49k curves take ~3 s on the M2 Pro.

**What could still be wrong.** Independent Gaussian noise overstates significance: real NEOWISE errors are correlated within a visit, and real turn-ons are high-SNR (the problem is confusion, not noise).

**Next.** Check whether the post-birth state is visible at 4.6 um at all (E-1B).

## E-1B · Is a young protostar visible at 4.6 um?

*2026-09-26 · verdict: **confirmed***

**Question.** Do Hi-GAL protostellar clumps (the post-transition state) show a W2 source above chance?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Excess W2 match rate (clump position minus 60" offset) > 0.05 for the youngest protostellar clumps.

**Test.** AllWISE crossmatch of 4,000 prestellar + 4,000 protostellar clumps (3", W2 SNR >= 5), with a 60" offset control; protostellar split by L/M quartile.

**Result.** Youngest quartile excess df_W2 = 0.26 (chance rate 4.4%); matched sources are bright (median W2 = 10.2). Prestellar clumps already show a 2.3% excess (vetoed).

**What could still be wrong.** Hi-GAL protostellar clumps are older and more luminous than a fresh second core: df_W2 is an optimistic upper bound for a true birth.

**Next.** Turn detectability into expected counts against the burst background (E-1A).

## E-1A · Expected births vs burst impostors

*2026-09-26 · verdict: **partial***

**Question.** Is the expected number of observable births large enough, and larger than bursts?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Y (observable births in 12 yr) >= 0.1 and exceeds the burst background B.

**Test.** Steady state N_T = N_proto * dt / tau_proto; Y = N_T * df_W2 * (1 - f_prehost); B = N_pre * f_hidden * dt / burst_interval * p_lift, over literature ranges.

**Result.** Y ~ 0.06-0.4 within 4 kpc (0.15-1.1 for all distances) but B ~ 1-53: signal/background 0.003-0.35. A W2 turn-on alone cannot claim a birth.

**What could still be wrong.** p_lift is a guess; Y ignores the literature result (L) that births are dark at 3-5 um for ~1e4 yr, which drives Y towards zero.

**Next.** Measure the empirical turn-on rate directly (E1).

## E1-pilot · NEOWISE turn-on pilot, 2,000 clumps

*2026-09-26 · verdict: **null***

**Question.** Do prestellar clumps show catalog-level W2 turn-ons above an offset control?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Prediction from E-1: excess ~1e-4-3e-3 per clump, i.e. undetectable in 2,000 clumps unless artefacts dominate.

**Test.** NEOWISE-R single exposures within 6" of 2,000 prestellar clumps (d <= 4 kpc) and offset positions; tracked-source classifier; injection-recovery on real steady light curves.

**Result.** 0 turn-ons on and off at every threshold; completeness 0.83-0.91 (SNR >= 10); 95% upper limit 1.7e-3 per clump. Hand-checking every near-candidate exposed 4 artefact classes, each now fixed and tested.

**What could still be wrong.** Coverage assumed complete every visit; faint (SNR < 10) turn-ons excluded by design.

**Next.** Scale to all 19,219 clumps within 4 kpc (E1-full).

## E1-full · Full turn-on search, 19,219 clumps

*2026-09-26 · verdict: **partial***

**Question.** Across every prestellar clump within 4 kpc, how many persistent W2 turn-ons appear 2014-2024?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Turn-ons occur at the burst-background rate; births contribute ~0.

**Test.** Same classifier and offset control as the pilot on 19,219 clumps (1.25M NEOWISE exposures on-source, 1.16M off).

**Result.** 4 turn-ons on-source vs 1 in the offset control (a flat-flux deblending artefact). Count excess alone is not significant (P = 0.19 for >= 4 of 5); the archival vetting (E3) separates them.

**What could still be wrong.** The confusion veto costs ~10-20% completeness in crowded fields.

**Next.** Vet every event against its archival history (E3).

## E3 · Archival vetting of every event

*2026-09-26 · verdict: **refuted***

**Question.** Is any turn-on a birth, i.e. with no young star at that position before?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** H_birth: no Spitzer/AllWISE source before onset. H_burst: a catalogued YSO was already there.

**Test.** Per event: NEOWISE coverage within 60", AllWISE 2010, Spitzer GLIMPSE I/II/3D, MIPSGAL, SPICY, SIMBAD, per-visit W2 image stacks and difference images.

**Result.** H_birth refuted for all 4: three were SPICY YSOs (flat-spectrum, Class II, Class I; each within 0.8" of the NEOWISE centroid) and one a known AllWISE YSO. Amplitudes 1.1-3.3 mag; G354.23+0.51 is a FUor-like 3.3 mag outburst with a 2-yr rise and 8-yr plateau. Best follow-up: G261.89-2.24, a faint red source with an 8-yr brightening episode.

**What could still be wrong.** Pre-existing-source checks depend on survey footprints (GLIMPSE misses |b| > 1 deg outside 3D).

**Next.** Use the events as a burst-rate measurement and compare with published intervals (E4).

## E4 · Burst-rate calibration against the literature

*2026-09-27 · verdict: **confirmed***

**Question.** Do the E1 turn-ons of catalogued YSOs occur at the published protostellar burst rate?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Implied interval tau = N_eff * dt_eff * completeness / k lies inside published confidence intervals (Zakri+2022, Park+2021, Contreras Pena+2024).

**Test.** SPICY YSOs within 3" of the 19,219 clumps; N_eff = hosts not steadily detected by NEOWISE (only these can register a turn-on); dt_eff = 8 yr; exact Poisson CI on k.

**Result.** 293 SPICY YSOs lie within 3" of the searched clumps (18 at offset positions); 192 were not steadily detected by NEOWISE. k = 3 turn-ons among them give tau = 445 yr (95% CI 152-2160 yr), consistent with: Zakri+2022 Class I + flat, Zakri+2022 all protostars, Park+2021 protostars (NEOWISE), Contreras Pena+2024 Class I FUor. Class I + flat: k = 2 of 99 hosts, tau = 345 yr (95% CI 95-2845 yr). Class II: k = 1 of 71 hosts, tau = 494 yr (95% CI 89-19518 yr). The Class II interval is far shorter than the ~112 kyr measured for Class II outbursts (Contreras Pena+2019): the one Class II host (SPICY 38399, W1-W2 = 2.7-3.2 in outburst) is probably an embedded protostar mislabelled in a crowded, extincted field.

**What could still be wrong.** Small k; hosts within 3" include chance alignments (still YSOs); SPICY misses Class 0 sources.

**Next.** E5: test whether SPICY classes of hosts inside cold clumps are reliable (SED refit with W1-W4 and Herschel), then faint (SNR < 10) turn-ons with forced photometry on unTimely epochal coadds.

## E5 · Are YSO classes reliable inside clumps?

*2026-09-27 · verdict: **partial***

**Question.** E4 found a Class II burst rate far above the published one. Are SPICY classes trustworthy for YSOs sitting inside cold Hi-GAL clumps?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** SPICY 'Class II' hosts of prestellar clumps are redder in IRAC [3.6]-[4.5] than Class II sources in protostellar clumps or in the field (i.e. extincted or mislabelled protostars).

**Test.** Fraction of Class II with [3.6]-[4.5] > 0.7 (the Gutermuth+2009 Class I colour condition), bootstrap 95% CI, for SPICY hosts within 3" of prestellar clumps, of protostellar clumps (both d <= 4 kpc), and a field sample (every 20th SPICY id, |b| < 1 deg).

**Result.** Red fraction of Class II: prestellar-clump hosts 0.71 (0.63-0.79) (n = 121), protostellar-clump hosts 0.84 (0.79-0.88) (n = 273), field 0.32 (0.30-0.34) (n = 2356). Clump hosts are 2.2-2.6x redder than the field, so the environment effect is confirmed; but prestellar hosts are not redder than protostellar ones, so a prestellar-specific mislabelling is refuted. The E4 Class II host (SPICY 38399, [3.6]-[4.5] = 1.72) sits at the 99th percentile of field Class II. SPICY classes are unreliable inside clumps; only the all-class E4 rate stands.

**What could still be wrong.** [3.6]-[4.5] mixes extinction (E([3.6]-[4.5]) ~ 0.13 A_K) with intrinsic envelope emission and 4.5 um shocked-H2 emission; a colour-colour test against the reddening vector is needed to separate them.

**Next.** E6: deredden hosts along the reddening vector ([3.6]-[4.5] vs [4.5]-[5.8]) to separate extinction from envelope emission; then forced photometry for faint turn-ons (unTimely).

## E6 · Extinction or envelope?

*2026-09-27 · verdict: **partial***

**Question.** E5 found clump-hosted YSOs strongly reddened. Is that dust in front of them (extinction) or their own envelopes (intrinsically younger objects)?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** H_ext: the extinction law is flat from 4.5 to 8 um (Indebetouw+2005: A_4.5 = A_5.8 = A_8.0 = 0.43 A_K, A_3.6 = 0.56 A_K), so clump hosts should be redder than the field in [3.6]-[4.5] but not in [4.5]-[5.8] (|delta median| < 0.1 mag). H_env: both colours shift.

**Test.** Median colour differences, clump Class II hosts minus field Class II, with bootstrap 95% intervals, for [3.6]-[4.5], [4.5]-[5.8] and [4.5]-[8.0]; implied A_K = delta([3.6]-[4.5]) / 0.13.

**Result.** Prestellar-clump hosts vs field: delta [3.6]-[4.5] = +0.30 (+0.23 to +0.43), delta [4.5]-[5.8] = +0.11 (+0.06 to +0.16), delta [4.5]-[8.0] = +0.03; implied A_K ~ 2.3. Protostellar-clump hosts: +0.48 / +0.20 / +0.13 (A_K ~ 3.7). The [3.6]-[4.5] shift is ~3x the [4.5]-[5.8] shift and [4.5]-[8.0] barely moves: the reddening is mostly extinction. The pre-registered cut (|delta [4.5]-[5.8]| < 0.1) is narrowly missed (+0.11), leaving a small residual that a dense-cloud extinction law or a modest envelope excess could explain. 36% of these 'Class II' hosts pass the full Gutermuth Class I colour cut vs 17% in the field.

**What could still be wrong.** Above A_K ~ 1 the IRAC extinction law flattens towards the Weingartner & Draine R_V = 5.5 model (Chapman+2009), which is not exactly flat from 4.5 to 5.8 um; PAH emission at 6.2 and 7.7 um enters [5.8] and [8.0] in bright regions.

**Next.** E7: faint (SNR < 10) turn-ons with forced photometry on unTimely epochal coadds, to reach below the catalog threshold where very young, low-luminosity sources would first appear.

## E7 · Faint turn-ons with forced photometry

*2026-09-27 · verdict: **superseded***

**Question.** Below the NEOWISE catalog threshold, do prestellar clumps show faint persistent brightenings that the catalog-level search (E1) could not see?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Rises (positive change point, onset 2015.5-2023.5) at clump positions exceed the offset-control rate once the delta-BIC threshold is set to a 1% false-positive rate on the offsets.

**Test.** Forced aperture photometry (r = 5.5", sky annulus 11-19") on every NEOWISE-R W2 frame at 685 'other'/'confused'/'turn_on' positions plus 500 random 'none' positions and their 60" offsets; per-visit medians; E0 change-point scan. Self-check: G354, G337 and G261 recovered blind (delta-BIC 5666 / 3010 / 414), offsets flat.

**Result.** Threshold delta-BIC >= 33 (1% false positives on offsets). Rises: 49 of 1170 clump positions vs 6 of 1174 offsets; 4 clump rises start from no detectable source. By E1 class: other 37, none 7, turn_on 4, confused 1. All 4 E1 turn-ons and G261 were recovered blind. After requiring no catalog detection within 3" before onset, the remaining 'from nothing' rises are the E1 events G337 and G59 (both with earlier Spitzer/AllWISE sources) and one sky-subtraction artefact (pre and post flux <= 0). The excess is variability of existing sources in clumps, the same population as E4; no new birth candidate.

**What could still be wrong.** Fixed-aperture photometry is blended with neighbours in crowded fields; a varying neighbour inside 5.5" mimics a rise. 'From nothing' uses pre-onset flux < 3 sigma, not a true upper limit.

**Next.** Superseded by E7b after an adversarial review: clump positions were preselected for variability while offsets were not, photometry errors ignored masks and visit systematics, and 'from nothing' did not require a post-onset detection.

## E7b · Faint rises, symmetric re-test

*2026-09-27 · verdict: **null***

**Question.** Correcting the three E7 flaws, is there a clump-associated excess of faint W2 rises, and does any rise start from nothing?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Pre-registered: confirmed only if the bootstrap 95% lower bound of the population-weighted rise rate (clumps minus offsets) is above zero, using rises confirmed with mask and uncertainty images.

**Test.** Both arms classified by the same E1 rule on their own detections and sampled per stratum (all variable, 500 none, 300 steady); rates weighted by stratum size. Frames with qual_frame = 0 or moon < 15 deg dropped; per-visit error = max(propagated, robust scatter) plus a systematic floor calibrated on offsets (median reduced chi^2 = 1); threshold = population-weighted 99th percentile of offsets (>= 20). Tier 2 remeasures every rise with L1b mask (fatal bits) and uncertainty images. Injection-recovery on real clump light curves. 'From nothing' needs pre-onset flux < 3 sigma, no catalog detection before onset, post-onset >= 5 sigma and a >= 5 sigma difference-image source.

**Result.** Threshold delta-BIC 47; floor s_abs = 0, s_rel = 0.028. Population-weighted rise rate (tier 2): clumps 183.1e-4 vs offsets 77.2e-4, difference 105.8e-4 (95% CI -12.1 to 227.6); tier 1: 120.0e-4 (-18.5 to 266.9). none: 7/500 vs 4/500 (Fisher p = 0.273). steady: 21/300 vs 1/300 (Fisher p = 0.000). variable: 31/689 vs 3/495 (Fisher p = 0.000). Clump rises with a pre-onset flux consistent with zero (|pre| < 3 sigma) and post >= 5 sigma (tier 2): 1; 4 more have a strongly negative baseline (<= -3 sigma), i.e. a failed sky model rather than an empty position, and are flagged unusable. Each is in the review desk. Reading: the excess is confined to positions that already host a catalog source; the 'none' stratum, the only place a source could appear from nothing, shows no significant excess (7/500 vs 4/500, p = 0.27). Because 'none' carries 91% of the population weight, the weighted difference is driven by it. Image-level validation: PSF sources injected into the frames of 40 clump positions. Tier 1 alone (photometry -> change point -> threshold, onset within 1 visit): 12: 98% (87-100); 13: 78% (62-89); 13.5: 42% (27-59); 14: 40% (25-57); 14.5: 32% (19-49); 15: 30% (17-47); 16: 0% (0-9). Joint recovery through the full candidate path (tier 1 AND tier 2 mask+uncertainty AND >= 5 sigma difference-image source), on a 12-position subsample: 13: 8/12 = 67% (35-90); 14: 5/12 = 42% (15-72); 15: 4/12 = 33% (10-65). The joint 50% point lies at aperture W2 ~ 13 (exact binomial 95% intervals in parentheses). Symmetric tier-1 negative audit (220 positions remeasured with mask + uncertainty images, both arms, every stratum): 0 cross the threshold at tier 2. Per-cell miss rates with exact 95% upper bounds: off/none 0/60 (<= 6%), off/steady 0/20 (<= 17%), off/variable 0/30 (<= 12%), on/none 0/60 (<= 6%), on/steady 0/20 (<= 17%), on/variable 0/30 (<= 12%). Population-weighted miss-rate upper bounds: clumps 6.8%, offsets 6.6%. Propagating the worst asymmetry, the true clump-minus-offset difference lies in [-552, 772] x 1e-4; the verdict rule (bootstrap lower bound > 0) is unchanged by it. Review catalog: 73 flagged clump positions, 0 birth-compatible after the 11-point archival checklist (0 pass every available check but lie outside a Spitzer/MIPSGAL footprint and are marked unverifiable); commonest first failure: pre_quiet 68, covered_pre 3, tier2_rise 1.

**What could still be wrong.** Aperture photometry still blends neighbours within ~6"; the offset arm samples the Galactic field, not clump backgrounds, so the FPR calibration is approximate at structured positions.

**Next.** Human review of every flagged candidate in the local review desk (scripts/serve_catalog.py); the far-IR/sub-mm two-epoch test remains the only route to a birth signal.

## E8 · Which dust hides a newborn at 4.6 um? (scenario)

*2026-09-27 · verdict: **partial***

**Question.** The literature says a newborn protostar is dark at 3-5 um. Is it the clump's own beam-averaged dust column (which Hi-GAL measures) or the unresolved inner envelope, and how much is simply distance?

**Hypothesis / prediction.** Per luminosity L in {1, 5, 25, 100} L_sun. H_dist(L): with zero extinction, fewer than half the clumps are close enough for the source to reach W2 = 14.3. H_clump(L): distance alone leaves a majority detectable but the clump column pushes the majority below the limit.

**Test.** Per prestellar clump (0 < d <= 4 kpc): A_4.5 from the catalogue surface density (Sigma -> N_H2 -> A_V -> A_K -> A_4.5, half the column for a central source); unextincted W2 scaled from the HOPS Class 0 median ([4.5] = 12.16 at 420 pc for 5.5 L_sun, F_4.5 ~ L); fraction detectable with and without clump extinction for L = 1, 5, 25, 100 L_sun.

**Result.** 19,328 prestellar clumps: median clump column A_V = 32 (10-90%: 11-130), i.e. A_4.5 at the centre 0.77 mag (90th pct 3.1). Model-dependent scenario, not a decomposition: the anchor is the OBSERVED [4.5] of 80 HOPS Class 0 sources scaled to 5.5 L_sun (median 12.45, 16-84% 10.82-14.62 mag), which already contains Orion envelopes, viewing geometry and foreground extinction, so the inner envelope is not separable and 'distance' and 'clump column' are shifts relative to an Orion-like Class 0. Fraction of clumps in which such a source would reach W2 = 14.3 (Monte Carlo over the anchor scatter): 1 L_sun: distance term alone 5% (16-84%: 5-6), with the clump column 3% (16-84%: 3-4); 5 L_sun: distance term alone 19% (16-84%: 17-22), with the clump column 11% (16-84%: 9-12); 25 L_sun: distance term alone 50% (16-84%: 46-54), with the clump column 31% (16-84%: 28-34); 100 L_sun: distance term alone 73% (16-84%: 69-77), with the clump column 53% (16-84%: 50-57). Reading: for the <= 5 L_sun newborns the collapse models predict, distance alone leaves almost none detectable; at 25 L_sun the clump column shifts a borderline majority below threshold, but the band width shows this is anchor-limited; the 100 L_sun figure is an upper bound above the 26% measured in E-1B, as expected since multiplicity and inner-envelope extinction are not modelled.

**What could still be wrong.** This is a scenario on an observed anchor, not a decomposition: the HOPS [4.5] values already contain envelope, geometry and Orion foreground extinction, so the inner-envelope term cannot be isolated and 'distance' vs 'clump column' are relative shifts. F_4.5 ~ L is crude (scattered/shocked light). A fresh newborn (no near-IR for ~2e4 yr per Masunaga & Inutsuka 2000) is fainter than any HOPS source, so all fractions are upper bounds. Sigma is beam-averaged (Hi-GAL 250 um beam ~18").

**Next.** Human review of every flagged candidate (review desk), then the far-IR/sub-mm two-epoch test that the literature identifies as the band where a birth first shows.

## Glossary

| term | definition |
|---|---|
| FHSC | First hydrostatic core: ~AU-scale, ~1000 K pressure-supported gas core that precedes H2 dissociation; lives ~10^2-10^3 yr. |
| second core / birth | Second hydrostatic core, formed after H2 dissociation triggers a second collapse; conventionally the birth of the protostar. |
| Class 0 | Youngest protostellar class: envelope mass > stellar mass, SED peaking in the far-IR/sub-mm (T_bol < 70 K). |
| T_bol | Bolometric temperature: temperature of a blackbody with the same mean frequency as the SED. |
| L_int | Internal luminosity of the central source only, excluding external heating. |
| VeLLO | Very low luminosity object: embedded protostar with L_int <= 0.1 L_sun (Dunham+2008). |
| PBRS | PACS Bright Red Source: Orion protostar with log(lambda F_lambda(70)/lambda F_lambda(24)) > 1.65 (Stutz+2013). |
| FUor / EXor | Long (decades) / short (months) accretion outbursts, named after FU Ori / EX Lup. |
| half-life | Kristensen & Dunham 2018's statistically inferred phase duration. |
| statistical lifetime | t_X = (N_X / N_ref) * t_ref; assumes steady state and that every X evolves into the reference class. |
| accretion burst | Episodic luminosity increase of an already-existing protostar (FUor/EXor-like); the main astrophysical false positive. |
| Hi-GAL | Herschel infrared Galactic Plane Survey (70-500 um, observed 2009-2013). Clump catalogue: Elia et al. 2021, IRSA table `higal360clump` (94,604 high-reliability rows). |
| prestellar (evol_flag=1) | Hi-GAL clump that is gravitationally bound and has no 70 um counterpart (49,289 clumps). |
| protostellar (evol_flag=2) | Hi-GAL clump with a 70 um counterpart (35,186 clumps). |
| high-reliability | Hi-GAL SED fitted with >=4 Herschel bands and T not at the 5/40 K grid edge (Elia+2021 Sec. 2); the IRSA table holds only these. |
| unbound starless (evol_flag=0) | Hi-GAL starless clump below the Larson-mass threshold (10,129 clumps). |
| W1 / W2 | WISE bands at 3.4 / 4.6 um; magnitudes are Vega (`w1mpro`, `w2mpro`). |
| AllWISE | WISE+NEOWISE-cryo coadded catalogue (epochs 2010-2011), IRSA `allwise_p3as_psd`; used as the ~Herschel-epoch baseline. |
| NEOWISE | Reactivated WISE W1/W2 survey 2013-12 to 2024-08, ~1-day visits every ~6 months; single exposures in IRSA `neowiser_p1bs_psd`. |
| E-1 | Feasibility experiment: expected number of observable turn-ons before building the pipeline. |
| E0 | Synthetic change-point calibration on WISE-like cadence (docs/EXPERIMENTS.md). |
| W2 match | An AllWISE source with w2snr >= 5 within 3 arcsec of the clump position (nearest kept). |
| offset control | Same targets shifted +60 arcsec in Dec; its W2 match rate f_off is the chance-alignment baseline. |
| f_on / f_off | Fraction of clumps with a W2 match at the clump position / at the offset position. |
| df_W2 | Detectability of the post-birth state = f_on - f_off for the youngest-quartile (lowest L_bol/M) protostellar clumps. |
| f_prehost | f_on - f_off for prestellar clumps: fraction already hosting a W2 source at the Herschel epoch (vetoed). |
| L/M | Clump bolometric luminosity over mass (L_sun/M_sun); low L/M = earlier evolutionary stage. |
| tau_pre / tau_proto | Lifetimes (Myr) of the Hi-GAL prestellar / protostellar clump phases. Steady state: N_pre/tau_pre = N_proto/tau_proto. |
| dt | Search window: 12 yr (Hi-GAL epoch 2010-2012 to NEOWISE end 2024-08, minus 2 epochs needed to confirm persistence). |
| N_T | Expected prestellar->protostellar transitions in the window: N_T = N_proto * dt / tau_proto. |
| Y | Expected observable W2 turn-ons: Y = N_T * df_W2 * (1 - f_prehost). |
| B | Expected accretion-burst background from protostars hidden in prestellar clumps: B = N_pre * f_hidden * dt / burst_interval. |
| NEOWISE visit | ~6-month bin of NEOWISE-R data counted from 2013-12-13 (visit 0) to 2024-08 (visit 21); a position is observed ~once per visit for ~1 day. |
| tracking | Following the source nearest the clump (centre = median of clean detections within 3"; keep detections within 2" of it) instead of a fixed aperture. |
| turn_on / steady / other / none | Catalog light-curve classes: turn_on = first detection after visit 3, >=3 detected visits, detected in >= min_post_frac of visits from onset, post-onset median SNR >= 10; steady = detected from the start in >= 80% of visits; other = sporadic; none = never detected. |
| confused | Light-curve class: would be a turn_on, but >= 50% of pre-onset visits already have any W2 detection (flags ignored) within 6" (~W2 PSF FWHM). |
| E1 full run | Turn-on search over all 19,219 prestellar clumps with 0 < d <= 4 kpc (plus 60" offset control), NEOWISE-R 2013-12 to 2024-08. |
| E3-lite vetting | Per-candidate archival history: NEOWISE coverage within 60", AllWISE, GLIMPSE I/II/3D, MIPSGAL, SPICY, SIMBAD (research/e3_vet_candidate.py). |
| coverage proxy | Number of NEOWISE detections of any source within 60" in a visit; > 0 means the position was imaged in that visit. |
| W2 stack | Median of up to 8 NEOWISE W2 single-exposure frames of one visit, reprojected to a common 64x64 grid at 1.5"/px (96" field); one brightness scale per target for all epochs. |
| difference image | W2 stack of a visit minus the median of the first three visits (2014-15); red = brighter, blue = fainter, white = no change. |
| burst rate (E1) | Completeness-corrected turn-on rate of pre-existing YSOs: k_on / (N searched * mean injection completeness), per clump over the NEOWISE window. |
| SPICY | Spitzer/IRAC Candidate YSO catalogue (Kuhn+2021, 117,446 sources); requires 3.6 and 4.5 um detections, so it misses the youngest Class 0 objects. |
| [3.6]-[4.5] | Spitzer IRAC colour (Vega mag) between 3.6 and 4.5 um; > 0.7 is part of the Gutermuth+2009 Class I criteria. Extinction adds E([3.6]-[4.5]) ~ 0.13 A_K (Indebetouw+2005). |
| SPICY host | A SPICY YSO within 3" of a clump position (E4/E5). |
| red fraction (E5) | Fraction of SPICY Class II sources with [3.6]-[4.5] > 0.7 mag; 95% CI from 2,000 bootstrap resamples. |
| [4.5]-[5.8] | Spitzer IRAC colour (mag); nearly extinction-free (A_4.5 = A_5.8 = 0.43 A_K, Indebetouw+2005), so it tracks intrinsic envelope emission. |
| Gutermuth Class I cut | [3.6]-[4.5] > 0.7 and [4.5]-[5.8] > 0.7 mag (simplified from Gutermuth+2009). |
| A_K | Extinction in the K band (mag); A_V ~ 10 A_K. Implied A_K (E6) = delta median([3.6]-[4.5]) / 0.13. |
| forced photometry (E7) | Aperture flux at a fixed sky position in every NEOWISE W2 frame (r = 2 px = 5.5", sky = median of a 4-7 px annulus), normalised to zeropoint 22.5 (m = 22.5 - 2.5 log10 f), median per visit. |
| rise (E7) | Forced-photometry light curve with a positive persistent step, onset in visits 3-19, and delta_BIC above the offset-calibrated threshold (1% false positives). |
| from nothing (E7) | A rise whose pre-onset median flux is < 3 robust sigma above zero, i.e. no source detected before the onset. |
| stratum (E7b) | E1 light-curve class group used for sampling: variable (other/confused/turn_on, all sampled), none (500 random), steady (300 random); each arm is classified on its own detections. |
| population-weighted rate (E7b) | Rise rate over all 19,219 positions of an arm: sum over strata of (N_stratum / N_total) * (rises / sampled); 95% CI by bootstrap within strata (2,000 resamples). |
| systematic floor | Per-visit error term sigma_sys^2 = s_abs^2 + (s_rel * F)^2 added in quadrature, calibrated so offset light curves have median reduced chi^2 = 1 for a constant model. |
| tier 1 / tier 2 (E7b) | Tier 1: forced photometry on quality-filtered intensity frames at every sampled position. Tier 2: every tier-1 rise remeasured with the L1b mask (fatal bits excluded) and uncertainty images; only tier-2 rises count. |
| fatal mask bits | WISE L1b mask bits 0-4, 9, 10-18 (Explanatory Supplement IV.4.a), plus 21, 26-28 (transient bad pixel, non-linearity, cosmic ray / spike) rejected here. |
| diff-image S/N | Peak of (median post-onset stack - median pre-onset stack) within 3" of the target over the robust std of the difference map beyond 9". |
| machine checklist | Nine automatic tests a birth start must pass: pre-onset coverage, forced pre-onset flux < 3 sigma, no catalog/Spitzer/AllWISE source within the 5.5" aperture before onset, tier-2 rise, post-onset >= 5 sigma, difference-image source >= 5 sigma, quiet offset twin; all pass = birth-compatible. |
| review decision | Human verdict recorded in reviews/reviews.jsonl: approve (birth start), refute or unsure, with reasoning, reviewer and tags; latest entry per candidate is current. |
| surf_dens | Hi-GAL clump surface density Sigma (g cm^-2), beam-averaged from the 250 um size and the SED mass. |
| A_4.5 (E8) | Extinction at 4.5 um from Sigma: N_H2 = Sigma / (2.8 m_H); A_V = 2 N_H2 / 1.87e21 (Bohlin+1978); A_K = 0.112 A_V (Rieke & Lebofsky 1985); A_4.5 = 0.43 A_K (Indebetouw+2005); a central source sees half. |
| W2_0(L, d) | Unextincted W2 of a Class-0-like source: 12.16 - 2.5 log10(L / 5.5 L_sun) + 5 log10(d / 0.42 kpc), scaled from the HOPS Class 0 median (Furlan+2016) assuming F_4.5 ~ L. |
| min_post_frac | Minimum fraction of visits (from onset onward) in which a turn_on source must be detected. |
| 95% UL (Poisson) | Upper limit on a rate with k=0 events in n trials: 3/n (from -ln 0.05 ~ 3). |
| sigma per exposure | Injected step amplitude in units of the per-exposure Gaussian noise sigma (synthetic E0 only). |
| delta_BIC | BIC(constant) - BIC(step); BIC = chi^2 + k ln n with k=1 (constant) or 3 (two levels + change index). Detection if >= 20 and step > 0. |
| detection rate | Fraction of simulated curves with delta_BIC >= threshold and positive step. |
| localization rate | Fraction of simulated curves detected AND with change index within +/-1 epoch (12 exposures) of truth. |
| SNR | Signal-to-noise ratio; SNR = 5 is a 5-sigma detection (`w2snr`). |
| binomial SE | Standard error of a fraction p over n trials: sqrt(p(1-p)/n). |
