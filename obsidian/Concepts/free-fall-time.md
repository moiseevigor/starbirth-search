# Free-fall time

> **Concept hub.** This note exists as a shared anchor — multiple paper summaries link here via their `key_concepts` frontmatter, so reading this gives the cross-paper view of one idea. Keep it short and authoritative. Long discussions go in paper notes, not here.

## Definition

The time for a uniform, pressureless sphere of density ρ to collapse to a point under its own gravity:

t_ff = √(3π / (32 G ρ))

[[enoch-2008]] (Eq. 8) writes it with ρ = ⟨n⟩ μ_p m_H and μ_p = 2.33.

## Why it matters

It is the natural unit for core and clump lifetimes. A measured lifetime of ~1 t_ff means near-free-fall (dynamic) evolution, while many t_ff means support by turbulence, magnetic fields or ambipolar diffusion (quasi-static). It is also the dynamical timescale of each collapse stage in theory.

## Cross-paper view

- **Core and clump lifetimes in t_ff units:** prestellar cores 1.5–3.9 t_ff, t_ff ≈ 1.4×10⁵ yr ([[enoch-2008]]: "argues for a dynamic, rather than quasi-static, core evolutionary scenario"); ~4 t_ff overall, ~t_ff for the densest cores at ≳10⁶ cm⁻³ ([[konyves-2015]]); massive starless phase 0.6–4.1 t_ff ([[battersby-2017]]); starless clumps below ~450 M⊙ outlive their ~0.8 Myr t_ff ([[svoboda-2016]]).
- **Collapse theory:** central collapse time 1.58 t_ff; the star reaches the Hayashi track after ~5 initial t_ff ([[larson-1969]]); second collapse starts at t₀ = 1.7526×10⁵ yr, close to t_ff = 1.77×10⁵ yr, and the second collapse itself lasts ~0.1 yr, the free-fall time at 10⁻⁷ g cm⁻³ ([[masunaga-inutsuka-2000]]).
- **Small scales:** second collapse t_ff ≈ 0.67 yr at 10⁻⁸ g cm⁻³; the first-core free-fall time (~5.5 yr) sets how long the new protostar stays buried ([[tomida-2013]]).
