# PACS Bright Red source

> **Concept hub.** This note exists as a shared anchor — multiple paper summaries link here via their `key_concepts` frontmatter, so reading this gives the cross-paper view of one idea. Keep it short and authoritative. Long discussions go in paper notes, not here.

## Definition

[[stutz-2013]] (Abstract): "we find 18 sources that have log λFλ70/λFλ24 > 1.65; we name these sources "PACS Bright Red sources", or PBRs."

PBRS (also written PBRs) are Class 0-like Orion protostars with very red SEDs (T_bol < 45 K) and large submillimetre fractions (L_smm/L_bol > 0.6%). [[stutz-2013]] concludes "the PBRs are most likely extreme Class 0 objects", with higher-than-typical envelope densities and infall rates. They are not first cores.

## Why it matters

PBRS are the reddest protostars that Spitzer largely missed, so they are the best-studied candidates for the youngest protostellar phase. They show the sample-selection effect of 24 µm-based surveys directly and give a short phase duration (~25 kyr) via [[statistical-lifetime]].

## Cross-paper view

- **Phase duration:** ~5% of Orion protostars → ~25 kyr at a 0.5 Myr protostellar lifetime; 5 kyr (Orion A, 1%) to 80 kyr (Orion B, 17%) ([[stutz-2013]]).
- **Physical cause:** no model gives 70/24 > 1.65 below log ρ1/(g cm⁻³) ~ −13.4 (Ṁ_env = 5.4×10⁻⁶ M⊙ yr⁻¹); inclination alone cannot produce the colour ([[stutz-2013]]). HOPS SED fits give a median ρ₁₀₀₀ of 1.2×10⁻¹⁷ g cm⁻³, twice the Class 0 median, and 14/19 fits with i ≥ 70° ([[furlan-2016]]).
- **Detectability:** 8 PBRS undetected at 24 µm ([[dunham-2014]]); only 9/19 have a measured 4.5 µm flux and 1/19 a K_s flux (vault-derived, [[furlan-2016]]).
- **Other links:** [[safron-2015]] uses PBRS Herschel photometry; HOPS 373, a PBR in the [[furlan-2016]] list, is a secular 850 µm variable in [[lee-2021]]; HOPS 404, from the PBRS sample, is a first-core candidate in [[young-2023]].

**Inconsistency to watch:** [[stutz-2013]] and [[dunham-2014]] give 18 PBRS; [[furlan-2016]] lists 19 PBRs (HOPS 169, 341, 354, 358, 359, 372, 373, 394, 397–405, 407, 409). The extra source should be traced before using PBRS counts.
