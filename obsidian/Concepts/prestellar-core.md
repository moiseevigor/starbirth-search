# Prestellar core

> **Concept hub.** This note exists as a shared anchor — multiple paper summaries link here via their `key_concepts` frontmatter, so reading this gives the cross-paper view of one idea. Keep it short and authoritative. Long discussions go in paper notes, not here.

## Definition

A prestellar core is a starless dense core (no embedded protostar detected) that is gravitationally bound and so expected to collapse and form stars. "Starless" is the observational statement; "prestellar" adds boundedness.

- Low-mass cores: "Prestellar" means bound according to a Bonnor–Ebert mass ratio ([[konyves-2015]]; a looser criterion gives the "candidate" set, a stricter one the "robust" set).
- Massive clumps: a starless Hi-GAL clump is pre-stellar if M(r) > 460 M⊙ (r/pc)^1.9 ([[elia-2017]]; see [[hi-gal-evolutionary-classification]]). BGPS/ATLASGAL use "starless clump candidate" or "quiescent" for the analogous stage ([[svoboda-2016]], [[lu-2024]]).

## Why it matters

It is the initial condition of protostellar collapse (the 10 K, ~1 M⊙ cores of [[larson-1969]], [[masunaga-inutsuka-2000]], [[bhandare-2020]], [[young-2023]]) and the reference population for [[statistical-lifetime]] ratios. Its lifetime in units of the [[free-fall-time]] is the main observational argument for dynamic versus quasi-static core evolution.

## Cross-paper view

- **Lifetime:** 4.5±0.8×10⁵ yr at n ≳ 2×10⁴ cm⁻³ ([[enoch-2008]]); ~0.5±0.1(±0.3) Myr ([[evans-2009]]); 1.2±0.3 Myr, ~4 t_ff ([[konyves-2015]]); massive clumps 0.2–1.7 Myr ([[battersby-2017]]) and τ_SCC ≈ 0.37 (10³ M⊙/M) Myr ([[svoboda-2016]]).
- **Density dependence:** t ∝ n^−1.2 ([[enoch-2008]]); a few ×10⁴ yr (~t_ff) at ≳10⁵–10⁶ cm⁻³ ([[konyves-2015]]).
- **Hidden protostars:** 15–25% of pre-Spitzer "starless" cores host low-luminosity protostars ([[dunham-2008]]); 18–38% in [[dunham-2014]]. FHSC candidates are found inside "starless" cores ([[enoch-2010]], [[pineda-2011]]).
- **Mass function:** CMF peak 0.45±0.2 M⊙ ([[konyves-2015]]); CMD slope α = −2.3±0.4 above 0.8 M⊙ ([[enoch-2008]]).
- **Variability null:** 0 of 190 starless 850 µm peaks vary over 4 yr ([[lee-2021]]); by contrast 18% of ATLASGAL "quiescent" clumps host a variable NEOWISE source ([[lu-2024]], contamination not analysed).
- In Hi-GAL, starless counts are upper limits on truly starless clumps ([[elia-2021]]).
