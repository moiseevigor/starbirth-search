# VeLLO (very low luminosity object)

> **Concept hub.** This note exists as a shared anchor — multiple paper summaries link here via their `key_concepts` frontmatter, so reading this gives the cross-paper view of one idea. Keep it short and authoritative. Long discussions go in paper notes, not here.

## Definition

A VeLLO is an embedded protostar, inside a dense core, with [[internal-luminosity]] L_int ≤ 0.1 L⊙. [[dunham-2008]] applies this cut to its c2d census: 15 of 50 embedded protostars with L_int ≤ 1 L⊙ are VeLLOs. The class was introduced for Spitzer discoveries in cores previously thought starless (Di Francesco et al. 2007).

## Why it matters

VeLLOs are the faintest known protostars. Many sit in cores catalogued as starless, so they bias [[prestellar-core]] counts and lifetimes. Their luminosities are far below steady [[accretion-luminosity]], which makes them key evidence for the [[luminosity-problem]] and [[episodic-accretion]]. They are also the main confusion case for [[first-hydrostatic-core]] searches.

## Cross-paper view

- **Census:** 15/50 (30%) of c2d low-luminosity embedded protostars; the luminosity distribution does not keep rising below 0.1 L⊙ ([[dunham-2008]]).
- **Nature:** heterogeneous — extremely young protostars, protostars in quiescent phases of episodic accretion, and proto-brown dwarfs; their mid-IR detections mean none can be an FHSC ([[dunham-2014]]).
- **Accretion tension:** IRAM 04191 L_int ~0.08 L⊙ vs ~2 L⊙ implied by its outflow ([[dunham-2008]]).
- **FHSC confusion:** Per-Bolo 58 (L_int ≈ 0.012 L⊙) fits an FHSC and an extreme VeLLO equally well ([[enoch-2010]]); L1451-mm is fainter than any c2d VeLLO ([[pineda-2011]]); PBRS candidate colours match Class 0 and VeLLOs rather than FHSC models ([[stutz-2013]]).
- **Variability:** 40/73 VeLLOs and low-luminosity sources (55%) are NEOWISE variables, more than half irregular ([[park-2021]]).
