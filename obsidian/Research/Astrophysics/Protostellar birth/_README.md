# Protostellar birth

Paper notes on the **physics of protostellar birth**: the isothermal collapse of a dense core, the first hydrostatic core, the H2-dissociation second collapse, and the first years to 10^5 yr of the second (stellar) core. The focus is on masses, radii, temperatures, lifetimes, the luminosity rise after second-core formation, and the predicted SED signatures.
Arc: [[larson-1969]] (1-D diffusion RHD, the original two-core picture) → [[masunaga-inutsuka-2000]] (1-D frequency-dependent RHD with SEDs and luminosity curves) → [[tomida-2013]] (3-D RMHD, the first year after second collapse) → [[bhandare-2020]] (1-D/2-D RHD over 0.5–100 M⊙ cloud cores, about 100–500 yr after second collapse) → [[young-2023]] (review, including first-core candidates and synthetic observables).
Concept hubs: [[first-hydrostatic-core]], [[second-collapse]].
