# Surveys and data

Paper notes on the survey data products and global normalisations used by the star-birth search: WISE/NEOWISE/unWISE time-domain data, YSO catalogues and SED model grids, and the Milky Way star-formation rate ([[chomiuk-povich-2011]] 1.9 ± 0.4 M⊙ yr⁻¹; [[licquia-newman-2015]] 1.65 ± 0.19 M⊙ yr⁻¹).
All Galactic SFR values here share a Kroupa-IMF / Starburst99 normalisation. Their quoted errors exclude common-mode IMF and calibration systematics (possibly a factor ~2–3 in absolute scale).
For the Hi-GAL clump-based SFR, see [[elia-2022]] in the Hi-GAL folder.
