# Variability and outbursts

Paper notes on protostellar/YSO variability and episodic-accretion outbursts: discovery cases (HOPS 383),
outburst-rate (recurrence-interval) estimates from multi-epoch Spitzer / WISE / NEOWISE / Gaia comparisons,
and mid-IR / sub-mm / near-IR monitoring surveys. Key hubs: [[accretion-outburst]], [[episodic-accretion]].
Recurrence intervals quoted here are Bayesian posteriors from counts of bursts k in N sources over baseline t — always read them with their confidence interval.
