# Youngest protostars

Paper notes on the **earliest observable objects** after the onset of collapse: first-hydrostatic-core (FHSC) candidates ([[enoch-2010]], [[pineda-2011]]), very low luminosity objects (VeLLOs) hidden in "starless" cores ([[dunham-2008]]), and the reddest far-IR-only protostars (PBRS, [[stutz-2013]]), plus the PPVI synthesis [[dunham-2014]].
Recurring threads: the L_int–F70 relation (70 µm flux as an internal-luminosity proxy), non-detection at 3–24 µm as a youth signature, the fraction of "starless" cores that actually host a source, and the luminosity problem / episodic accretion.
Concept hubs: [[first-hydrostatic-core]], [[internal-luminosity]], [[luminosity-problem]], [[episodic-accretion]], [[class-0-protostar]].
