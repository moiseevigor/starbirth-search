# Tag Taxonomy — starbirth-search vault

Same six-axis scheme as the shared math-agents vault (adapted copy). Every note carries:
one `#agent/*`, one `#user/*`, one `#domain/*`, one-to-many `#topic/*` (substance test,
typically 4-10), one `#status/*`, plus a content type (`paper`, `synthesis`, `concept`).
Frontmatter `domain:` and `status:` must mirror the tags. English only; translated text marked `[trans.]`.

## Axis 4 — `#topic/*` (multi-tag; reuse before invent; register here first)

### Protostellar birth physics (registered 2026-09-26)
- `#topic/protostellar-collapse` — gravitational collapse of dense cores to protostars (RHD/RMHD models).
- `#topic/first-hydrostatic-core` — FHSC formation, lifetime, observational signatures, candidates.
- `#topic/second-collapse` — H2-dissociation-driven second collapse and second-core (protostar) formation.
- `#topic/radiation-hydrodynamics` — RHD/RMHD numerical methods as the apparatus of a result.
- `#topic/protostar-observational-signatures` — predicted SED/luminosity/line signatures of the youngest objects.

### Early evolutionary stages & statistics
- `#topic/prestellar-cores` — starless/prestellar cores and clumps, their identification and properties.
- `#topic/class-0-protostars` — the youngest protostellar class (incl. PBRS, VeLLOs).
- `#topic/evolutionary-lifetimes` — statistical phase lifetimes (starless, Class 0/I, clump stages).
- `#topic/embedded-low-luminosity-protostars` — VeLLOs, hidden protostars in "starless" cores, L_int–F70 relations.
- `#topic/massive-clumps` — high-mass star-forming/starless clumps (BGPS, ATLASGAL, Hi-GAL).
- `#topic/galactic-star-formation-rate` — Milky Way SFR and star-birth rate estimates.

### Surveys & catalogs
- `#topic/hi-gal` — Herschel infrared Galactic Plane Survey and its catalogs.
- `#topic/wise-neowise` — WISE/NEOWISE/unWISE/unTimely data and processing.
- `#topic/spitzer-surveys` — c2d, GLIMPSE, MIPSGAL, HOPS Spitzer data.
- `#topic/vvv-survey` — VVV/VVVX/VIRAC near-IR Galactic variability survey.
- `#topic/sed-modeling` — YSO SED model grids and fitting.
- `#topic/yso-catalogs` — YSO candidate catalogs (SPICY etc.), used for vetoes.
- `#topic/source-distances` — kinematic/other distances of Galactic sources.

### Time-domain & variability
- `#topic/protostellar-variability` — mid-IR/sub-mm variability of YSOs.
- `#topic/accretion-outbursts` — FUor/EXor-type episodic accretion bursts, rates, recurrence.
- `#topic/time-domain-surveys` — multi-epoch survey design, cadence, transient/variable searches.
- `#topic/submm-monitoring` — sub-mm monitoring (JCMT Transient Survey).

### Consolidated from the 2026-09-26 ingestion batch (agent proposals merged; registered 2026-09-26)
- `#topic/accretion-luminosity` — luminosity from infall/accretion onto the protostar; post-birth luminosity evolution and burst energetics.

- `#topic/protostellar-outflows` — first-/second-core outflows, molecular outflows and jets as stage discriminants.
- `#topic/herschel-protostar-surveys` — HOPS / Herschel Gould Belt / PACS 70-160 um protostar censuses.

- `#topic/survey-artefacts-and-contamination` — quality flags, nebulosity, blends, AGB/extragalactic contaminants in source/variability searches.
- `#topic/radiative-transfer` — dust radiative-transfer models of YSO SEDs/envelopes.
