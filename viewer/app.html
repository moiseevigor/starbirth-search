Archival search · Hi-GAL × WISE/NEOWISE × Spitzer

Ten years of watching starless clouds for a star to switch on

We followed 19,219 Herschel "prestellar" clumps within 4 kpc through every NEOWISE visit from 2014 to 2024. Four of them gained a new 4.6 µm source. In every case Spitzer or AllWISE had already seen a young star at that spot: these are accretion outbursts, not births. Collapse models agree that a newborn protostar stays dark at these wavelengths for about 10⁴ years.
